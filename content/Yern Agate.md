# 31-07-2026

For an Aid Token, write up a description of an unusual dream that your character has had multiple times, but doesn't understand the meaning...

Yern is late, again, but the sounds of the harbour and the glint of the sea seen from the window of his room holds him still. For just another long moment. Until he's really late and in a mad dash to dress. Jeans that don't really pass the sniff test, but nothing else cleaner. Day old bread that does nothing to fill the relentless fire of his hunger. A guitar wiped, cleaned and just about to be put in its case but now instead to pause, to tune, to strum, to march an old shanty redressed as punk up and down the fretboard.
His room fades into window and salt, an old stage is there instead. His jeans are still dirty, but it doesn't matter as much because he's sweat and tremor now. The guitar reverberates through the not entirely empty hall. Just an old man, an old woman, their wolf and their raven. Cruel, lean faces.
'Play louder, else I sleep', says the woman and closes her eyes.
Yern plays until his fretboard his red, wet and reeks of salt. He plays until the sea sighs in. Until the wolf pads forward and rests its great head on the stage. It turns its head to the side and Yern sees the empty socket where the raven has stolen its third eye.
The music is faultless, and so Yern understands it must be a dream, but it holds him. He needs to make her hear. His hands sting against the metal strings. The wolf's breath is a gale on his sweaty face. The raven stares. The old man stares. The old woman sleeps; so Yern screams his song into the hall to wake her. The wolf swallows his song.
"I hear nothing. This skald is weak," says the man, he wakes the woman with ease. They get up, leave the room.
The wolf, with Yern's stolen song lolling in its mouth, follows its mistress home.
The raven remains, takes wing and finds poise on the head of Yern's guitar.
"Your song is wrong, I'll teach you," says the raven and with tender care for its claws it walks up Yern's arm to his shoulder. "Lean close, I shall whisper."
Yern leans in, and the raven steals his eye.
