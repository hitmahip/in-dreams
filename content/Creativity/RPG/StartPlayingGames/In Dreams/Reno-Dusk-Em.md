Reno had dreamed of the crash more times than she could count. Only this time, she wasn’t the frightened little girl strapped into the back seat. She was older. Old enough to understand. Old enough to know exactly what was about to happen. Her pulse pounded as dread settled over her like a suffocating blanket. She recognized every bend in the road, every mile marker, every flash of lightning that illuminated the storm. For several long moments she couldn’t tell whether she was still hearing the rain outside…or the echo of twisted metal from a nightmare she could never escape.
